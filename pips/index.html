How to play

  • Throw up to three times a turn. Press Throw and the dice tumble into the tray. Whatever lands face up is your roll.
  • Hold dice between throws. Click a die in the tray (or below it) and it moves to the rail. Click it again to put it back.
  • Then score one box. Every box gets used once, even for zero. Thirteen turns, thirteen boxes.
  • Upper section: add up the dice showing that number. Reach 63 for a +35 bonus.
  • Lower section: three or four of a kind score the sum of all dice. Full house 25, small straight 30, large straight 40, five of a kind 50. Chance is any sum.
  • Extra five of a kind: +100 if you already scored 50 there. It then acts as a joker: use its upper box if open, otherwise any lower box at full value.
